Round-screen rules

  1. Safe square. Dense text lives in the inscribed square (71% of the diameter). Never put words in the corners.
  2. The rim is for status. The outer 24 px ring holds arcs, curved labels and page dots only.
  3. Lists bend. Each row's left inset follows the circle. The center row is the focused one and is larger.
  4. Fisheye grids. Honeycomb icons shrink toward the edge so the circle never cuts an icon in half.
  5. 7 mm targets. The screen is about 259 ppi, so a tap target is at least 64 px on the real 360 px screen (72 on this 400 px grid).
  6. IPS, not AMOLED. Black does not save battery here — the backlight is always on. Stay dark for contrast and night use; no always-on face. Wrist-down = screen off.
  7. Every scroll has a rotary path. Anything you can swipe through, you can also turn through, so small screens need fewer taps.

Hardware: LTE, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, rough GPS. The heart sensor is not used — no screen depends on it. Hold home = lock: that is the system's, the launcher cannot change it. Borrowed from watchOS (honeycomb, crown zoom), One UI Watch (round quick panel), Wear OS (curved list), Zepp OS / Garmin (one-metric tiles).
